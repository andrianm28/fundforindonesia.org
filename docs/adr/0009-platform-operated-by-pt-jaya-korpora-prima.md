# The platform is operated by PT Jaya Korpora Prima, not by the foundation

Fund for Indonesia is registered to PT Jaya Korpora Prima so that every platform in the group sits under the same company. Yayasan Indonesia Emas Merdeka is not the platform owner; it is the first partner organisation running programs on it. The operator holds the payment provider's merchant account, employs the verifiers and admins, and earns the platform fee. It does not own campaign funds.

## Consequences

- The domain now distinguishes the Platform Operator from a Partner Organisation. Kind Authorisation for zakat and waqf attaches to a partner, never to the operator, because the operator is a company and those kinds require a licensed institution.
- The merchant account and the collection bank account are separate configurable things. Funds settle into the operator's provider balance, and the collection account each kind sweeps into is configured per kind and may belong to a different legal entity.
- Indonesian rules on collecting public donations require the permit holder to be a foundation or an association, not a limited company. So donations will most likely need to be collected in a partner's name even though the operator holds the merchant account. Counsel must confirm the arrangement before launch; the separation above is what makes either arrangement expressible in the system.
