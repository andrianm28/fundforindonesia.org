# No donor tip

Donors pay exactly the campaign amount and nothing else. We considered an optional tip on top of the donation to fund operations, which is common on Indonesian donation platforms, and rejected it. A tip adds a second amount to every checkout, complicates the provider fee split (the fee is charged on the combined total, so some of the tip's fee would fall on the campaign or vice versa), and gives donors a decision to make at the exact moment the flow should be shortest. Operations are funded by the platform fee instead, which is a single number shown openly on the campaign page.
